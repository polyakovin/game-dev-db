# Practical learning paths: source review

Checked public primary pages on 2026-10-20. The twelve lessons and their translations are independently written for Game Dev DB. Their station, rescue-tool, battery-bag, robot and dash scenarios are illustrative proposals, not executed game implementations or reported user research. No external chapters, exercises, code or images are republished.

| Lesson | Primary reference and use |
| --- | --- |
| `level-blockout` | [The Level Design Book: Blockout](https://book.leveldesignbook.com/process/blockout) and [Metrics](https://book.leveldesignbook.com/process/blockout/metrics): playable geometry and movement dimensions before final art. The 12/20 m warehouse calculation is our own example. |
| `level-wayfinding` | [Wayfinding](https://book.leveldesignbook.com/process/blockout/wayfinding): player orientation and route information, with exploration preserved. Station signage is original. |
| `teaching-a-mechanic` | [Patrick Holleman's Reverse Design: Super Mario World](https://thegamedesignforum.com/features/RD_SMW_1.html): analyzing individual challenges and their sequences. Our pulse-platform rooms do not reproduce Nintendo levels or the book's examples. |
| `choice-cost` | [Ian Schreiber, Level 3](https://gamebalanceconcepts.wordpress.com/2010/07/21/level-3-transitive-mechanics-and-cost-curves/): common cost models and their limits. Rescue-tool thresholds are calculated independently. |
| `controlled-randomness` | [Level 4](https://gamebalanceconcepts.wordpress.com/2010/07/28/level-4-probability-and-randomness/) and [Level 5](https://gamebalanceconcepts.wordpress.com/2010/08/04/level-5-probability-and-randomness-gone-horribly-wrong/): probability models and experienced randomness. Battery probabilities and the six-empty-crate bag bound are independent calculations. |
| `progression-pacing` | [Level 7](https://gamebalanceconcepts.wordpress.com/2010/08/18/level-7-advancement-progression-and-pacing/): advancement and pacing. Station damage and income tables are original, with explicit simplifying assumptions. |
| `npc-perception` | [Martin Walsh, Game AI Pro 2 chapter 28](https://www.gameaipro.com/GameAIPro2/GameAIPro2_Chapter28_Modeling_Perception_and_Awareness_in_Tom_Clancy%27s_Splinter_Cell_Blacklist.pdf): perception, awareness, consistency and feedback. Our drone is a deliberately simpler independent model. |
| `npc-action-selection` | [Dawe et al., Game AI Pro chapter 4](https://www.gameaipro.com/GameAIPro/GameAIPro_Chapter04_Behavior_Selection_Algorithms.pdf) and [David Graham, chapter 9](https://www.gameaipro.com/GameAIPro/GameAIPro_Chapter09_An_Introduction_to_Utility_Theory.pdf): algorithm choices, common utility scales and inertia. Robot actions and score values are original. |
| `npc-pathfinding` | [Amit Patel, Red Blob Games](https://www.redblobgames.com/pathfinding/a-star/introduction.html): graph search, edge costs and heuristics. The five-edge graph and its route sums are original. |
| `running-a-playtest` | [The Level Design Book: Playtesting](https://book.leveldesignbook.com/process/blockout/playtesting): question-led sessions and observation. The station notes are explicitly hypothetical. |
| `gameplay-acceptance` | [Cucumber Gherkin reference](https://cucumber.io/docs/gherkin/reference/) for Given/When/Then, plus [Godot debugging overview](https://docs.godotengine.org/en/stable/tutorials/scripting/debug/overview_of_debugging_tools.html) for diagnostic tools. The dash contract is engine independent; the lesson does not describe Godot engine unit tests as game-project tests. |
| `game-accessibility` | [Game Accessibility Guidelines: Basic](https://gameaccessibilityguidelines.com/basic/) and [Xbox guideline 117](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/xbox/accessibility/xbox-accessibility-guidelines/117): input, redundant information and visual-distraction options. The pumping-station review is original and not a certification claim. |

The Level Design Book and Game AI Pro have their own reuse restrictions; linking and discussing general techniques does not relicense their text. Game Dev DB's CC BY 4.0 applies to its original explanations and translations. Source access was checked, but no claim is made that all linked books or courses were completed. Example arithmetic was separately checked; the proposed games and playtests were not executed.
