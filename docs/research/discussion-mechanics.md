## Already covered or outside the addition

Voice/audio, point-and-click, detective, children and education catalogs already contain their requested material. The combined children/education reference remains available as a compatibility archive. The two external mechanic indexes already include their imported source entries and references. Earlier site setup, navigation, theme, resource, jam, practice and lens discussions concern other content/features and introduce no further missing mechanic list.

Similar actions remain in multiple catalogs when their context differs. The microphone catalog intentionally refines input mechanisms already mentioned within the broader voice/audio reference. Puzzle, metroidvania and metroidbrainia overlap without being interchangeable. The ten perception ideas are hypothetical design tools; they do not claim to reproduce real-world instruments.

## Content handling

Authored groups become catalog families; individual records become variants. Input/action, application, recognition requirement, hardware capability and limitation fields remain in the localized text. Original IDs are normalized under each new catalog namespace. Source identifiers and associations are mapped to public HTTPS references. Modifiers, classification boundaries, practical checks and illustrative combinations remain supplementary notes. Source lists link external material without importing article bodies or media.

Primary documentation was rechecked for representative technical boundaries: [Pointer Events](https://www.w3.org/TR/pointerevents3/), [UI Events](https://www.w3.org/TR/uievents/), [Pointer Lock](https://www.w3.org/TR/pointerlock-2/), [AppKit trackpad events](https://developer.apple.com/library/archive/documentation/Cocoa/Conceptual/EventOverview/HandlingTouchEvents/HandlingTouchEvents.html), [Android position sensors](https://developer.android.com/develop/sensors-and-location/sensors/sensors_position), [haptics APIs](https://developer.android.com/develop/ui/views/haptics/haptics-apis), and [CameraX configuration](https://developer.android.com/media/camera/camerax/configuration). Narrative and puzzle examples were cross-checked against [Ren’Py choices](https://www.renpy.org/doc/html/menus.html), [ink documentation](https://www.inklestudios.com/ink/web-tutorial/), [NetHack’s guide](https://www.nethack.org/v367/Guidebook.html) and [Nikoli’s index](https://www.nikoli.co.jp/en/puzzles/). Other original source links and their stated scope are retained; this is not independent validation of every linked game example. The Dominion publisher page did not load through the research tool in this audit.

The engagement catalog is an analytical taxonomy, not diagnostic guidance or proof that any one pattern causes a disorder. Its context retains [WHO’s distinction](https://www.who.int/news-room/questions-and-answers/item/addictive-behaviours-gaming-disorder) between engagement and clinically significant impairment. Hardware effects and hypothetical game ideas have not been tested on physical devices or with players.

## Verification record

Node 24 verification passed on an isolated copy containing only this change: `npm run verify`, `npm run format:check` and all 267 Playwright tests. The complete shared working version also passed 277 browser tests, providing a compatibility check against the parallel knowledge-content changes. Russian and English production-preview pages were inspected at 1440px and 390px in light and dark appearance; fonts loaded, requests succeeded and no horizontal overflow was observed.

All 3,941 new variant identities and names were compared with the imported lists. Every original RPG grind family remains unchanged. English text was prepared with Google Translate following explicit user authorization, then reviewed with terminology corrections for cards, discards, dialogue lines, runs, leading targets and hardware constraints. Structural validation, untranslated-text checks and numeric/negation review support the translation review; they do not constitute independent verification of every game example. No translation service is used by the published site. Hardware and playtest limits described above still apply.
