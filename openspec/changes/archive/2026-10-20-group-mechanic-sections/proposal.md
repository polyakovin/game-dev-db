# Proposal

## Why

The RPG grind contents repeats its four layer names in every section link. Separate group subtitles make the hierarchy easier to scan and let section names stay concise.

## What Changes

- Add optional bilingual section group labels and apply them to the eighteen RPG grind sections.
- Show each group once in the contents, article and section selector, with shorter section titles in RU and EN.
- Preserve grouping in Markdown and additive v1 JSON metadata while retaining all structural identities and URLs.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities

None.

### Modified Capabilities

- `mechanics-reference`: explicit localized section grouping in reading, filtering and exports.

## Impact

Mechanic data localization, validation, renderer, search visibility, styles and Markdown generation; focused browser coverage and content documentation. No dependency or route changes.
