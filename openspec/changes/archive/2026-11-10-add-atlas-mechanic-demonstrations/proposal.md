## Why

Mechanic cards explain behavior with annotated screenshots, but a still image cannot show the order of a warning, response and recovery. Reuse the owner's Boss Fight Atlas characters and teaching approach directly on the matching mechanic pages.

## What Changes

- Add deterministic SVG demonstrations for charge, sweep, ground slam, summon and gap volley, each with its own threat geometry and player response.
- Reuse the atlas's articulated boss/player artwork and stone arena with attribution and pinned source provenance.
- Provide RU/EN phase explanations, pause, replay, seeking and speed controls, reduced-motion handling and static access.
- Keep the existing screenshot accessible and add the demonstration explanation to common JSON/Markdown exports.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities
- `mechanic-demonstrations`: Rule-specific animated scenes within existing mechanic cards.

### Modified Capabilities

None. Existing routes, catalog identity, screenshots and v1 fields remain compatible.

## Impact

Touches mechanic presentation, shared knowledge enrichment, a demonstration registry/model/controller, scoped CSS and tests. Animation Lab remains a separate local 3D viewer. The initial registry covers the five canonical atlas reference scenes; unrelated mechanics retain their authored screenshots.
