# Proposal

## Why

The 329-direction map still has 301 unfinished topics after integration of the existing catalogs. Fill every remaining direction with useful bilingual reference content, starting with widely reusable movement, combat, shooting, exploration, progression, strategy and puzzle mechanics.

## What Changes

- Connect existing substantive topic-specific families where available; otherwise author original RU/EN overviews: a core action/decision loop, at least four concrete variants, a hypothetical design scenario and a specific prototype check.
- Publish related overviews in coherent catalogs for the 32 map groups with primary-source context, scope limits and links to deeper existing references where relevant.
- Let a direction link directly to its authored family inside a group catalog while retaining its stable direction identity and expected standalone catalog ID. Existing full standalone references remain the preferred target.
- Derive readiness from the actual registered catalog and target family; no empty or missing target becomes ready.
- Keep all new material in the existing HTML, Markdown, JSON and knowledge-export pipeline. Preserve every existing article URL and anchor.
- Land content in useful batches in editorial order, with all 329 directions covered at completion. The order reflects reuse across game designs, not a claim of measured genre market share.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities

None.

### Modified Capabilities

- `mechanics-reference`: complete direction coverage, group overviews and specific family targets for direction discovery.

## Impact

Group source catalogs and an audited list of reused existing families, their registry, source-order registration, direction target metadata, the direction resolver/card links, proportional validators/tests and coverage documentation. No new runtime service or dependency. Examples are original hypothetical designs; cited developer/standard documentation supports specific techniques and hardware boundaries rather than proving the editorial taxonomy.
