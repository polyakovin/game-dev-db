# Proposal

## Why

The combined children's educational catalog mixes an audience with a learning purpose. Readers need a children's play reference and an educational reference covering learners of different ages, with missing forms added to each.

## What Changes

- Publish bilingual `children` and `education` catalogs with distinct scope notes, original additions, hypothetical examples and primary sources.
- Replace the combined discovery card with two cards; preserve its HTML anchors, original content, JSON identity and Markdown endpoint as a compatibility archive with visible links to both successors.
- Preserve original family and variant identities when reusing content; retain all 749 original variants in education.
- Keep v1 exports additive and generated from bilingual source data.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities

None.

### Modified Capabilities

- `mechanics-reference`: independent children's and educational references, expanded coverage and compatibility migration.

## Impact

Bilingual mechanic JSON, catalog discovery and rendering, v1 records, manifest counts, content documentation and browser regression tests. No new dependency or runtime service.
