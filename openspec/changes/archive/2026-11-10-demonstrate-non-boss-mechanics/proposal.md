## Why

The user wants the atlas characters and teaching format to explain general
game mechanics. The first version repeated boss encounters on boss cards.

## What changes

Replace the five encounter demonstrations with original scenes for precise
jumps, parrying, detection, independent movement and aim, and asymmetric
roles. Reuse the artwork, deterministic player and phase explanations.
Boss cards return to their illustrated presentation. Existing routes and
screenshots stay in place. The new scenes have paired RU/EN copy and use the
existing additive demonstration descriptor in common v1 exports.
