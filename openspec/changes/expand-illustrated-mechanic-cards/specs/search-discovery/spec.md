## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Prioritized illustrated mechanic coverage
Every active canonical mechanic SHALL receive a concise RU/EN card with an inspected real-game or application screenshot, attributed source and contextual marker tooltips. Production SHALL follow a documented editorial order based on broad usefulness. Incomplete image research SHALL not be represented as completed coverage. Existing URLs and full reference exports SHALL remain compatible.

#### Scenario: Start with broadly reusable mechanics
- **WHEN** cards are authored or published incrementally
- **THEN** common movement, combat, interaction and core systems precede specialized variants, with explicit remaining canonical coverage

#### Scenario: Inspect a completed card
- **WHEN** a reader opens an authored card in either language
- **THEN** its short title, definition and marked screenshot use the reviewed single-screen and minimal layouts, with keyboard/touch tooltips and native titles without JavaScript
- **AND** concise advice and example links appear only when space permits while full sources remain in machine formats

#### Scenario: Reject an incomplete illustration
- **WHEN** a card lacks localization, attribution, a valid local image, valid crop or an in-frame contextual marker
- **THEN** publication validation fails

#### Scenario: Audit complete coverage
- **WHEN** the rollout is marked complete
- **THEN** every active canonical mechanic appears once in the production inventory and has an authored, validated bilingual illustrated card
